# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

<!-- Synthetic sample document for the basic-chatbot demo. Contoso is a fictional company. -->

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted
between 31 and 90 days late need director approval. Expenses older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Receipts

Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over EUR 25. For expenses of EUR 25 or less, a receipt is
optional but the description must say what was purchased and why. Credit card statements alone are not
accepted as receipts.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are reimbursed up to EUR 90 per attendee, including tax and tip. List every attendee's
name and company in the expense report. Alcohol is reimbursed only at client meals and never exceeds
30% of the bill.

## Home office equipment

Employees approved for hybrid or remote work can claim up to EUR 500 every two years for home office
equipment such as a chair, desk, monitor or headset. Laptops and phones are provided by IT and can't be
claimed. Equipment bought with this allowance belongs to the employee.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll if approved before the 20th of the month.
Otherwise they are paid with the following payroll.
