# Contoso Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

<!-- Synthetic sample document for the basic-chatbot demo. Contoso is a fictional company. -->

## Hybrid schedule

The default arrangement is hybrid: at least 2 days per week in the office. Teams agree on shared
"anchor days" so colleagues overlap. Managers can approve a different pattern for individual roles.

## Fully remote work

Fully remote work requires approval from both your manager and HR, and is reviewed every 12 months.
Fully remote employees must live in a country where Contoso has a legal entity: Sweden, Germany,
the Netherlands, Ireland or Bulgaria.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work temporarily from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with
manager approval. Longer stays need an HR and tax review because they can create tax or immigration
obligations. Working from a country under international sanctions is never allowed.

## Equipment and security

Use only Contoso-managed laptops for company work. Connect through the company VPN on any network that
isn't your home network. Don't print confidential documents at home. For home office furniture and
peripherals, see the home office equipment allowance in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone on working days,
unless they're on leave or have agreed different hours with their manager.
