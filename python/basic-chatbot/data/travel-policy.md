# Contoso Business Travel Policy

<!-- Synthetic sample document for the basic-chatbot demo. Contoso is a fictional company. -->

## Scope

This policy applies to all Contoso employees and contractors who travel on company business.
It was last revised on 1 September 2026 and replaces the 2024 travel policy.

## Pre-approval

Domestic trips with a total estimated cost under EUR 1,500 need approval from your direct manager only.
International trips, and any trip estimated at EUR 1,500 or more, also need approval from your department
director. Submit the request in the Travel Portal at least 14 days before departure. Trips booked without
pre-approval are not reimbursed except in documented emergencies.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
Business class requires written approval from a vice president and is limited to flights over 10 hours.
Always book through the Travel Portal so the company travel insurance applies.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit, excluding taxes, depends on the city:

- London, New York, Zurich: EUR 280 per night
- Paris, Amsterdam, Stockholm, Munich: EUR 220 per night
- All other cities: EUR 160 per night

If no hotel is available within the limit, book the cheapest available option within 2 km of the office
or event venue and attach a screenshot of the alternatives to your expense report.

## Ground transport

Use public transport where practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for trips to and from the
airport, for travel between 22:00 and 06:00, or when carrying more than 15 kg of equipment.
Car rental requires manager pre-approval and is limited to compact or mid-size cars.

## Per diem

Meals are covered by a daily per diem instead of receipts: EUR 60 per day for domestic travel and
EUR 75 per day for international travel. On the first and last day of a trip, the per diem is 50%.
